# BlindBoxAI inbound alpha acquisition — October 2026

## Goal and boundaries
Convert useful public collectible research into **qualified early-access opt-ins**, and only then test willingness to pay. Keep existing EPN/Amazon disclosure and destination rules unchanged. No cold automation, mass unsolicited posts, unaudited prices, auto-spend, or autonomous social publishing.

## What exists vs what is proposed
- **Live code on merge/deploy:** /pro early-access landing, server-only Supabase waitlist, privacy notice, attribution, plus a discoverable /tools/resale-margin browser-local scenario calculator in the XML sitemap.
- **Existing public service:** reviewed completed-sale evidence and Mr. Know It All at /ask (subject to actual evidence availability).
- **Not yet live:** alerts, batch valuation, CSV exports, account/saved collections, billing, alpha invitations. Planned $9/month is a hypothesis, not MRR.

## Human decision gap
Most price comparisons stop at asking prices. A reseller actually needs: (1) documented sold comps and freshness; (2) all-in entry/exit economics; (3) authenticity/return risk; (4) a clear reason **not** to buy; (5) a repeatable watch-and-recheck workflow. Avoid claiming competitive superiority without source-backed analysis.

## Measurable funnel
1. Publish one focused, useful evidence-backed question/page around a collector decision. Keep unknown prices unknown.
2. Share owned-domain page URLs using relevant attribution. Never distribute raw eBay links.
3. On research pages invite users to the **free margin calculator + alpha** at /pro.
4. Track first-party landing, owned waitlist confirmation, and outbound EPN clicks separately; network-confirmed commission is revenue, clicks are not.
5. Analyze qualified opt-ins by source before scaling. Use existing owner-only telemetry. Never put subscriber email in analytics.

## Copy that can be adapted for owner-reviewed publishing
“Would you buy this collectible at today's asking price? Before deciding, check reviewed completed sales, then subtract real fees and shipping. Try the free BlindBoxAI resale-margin check and opt in for early access: https://www.blindboxai.com/pro?utm_source=owned_social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=alpha_launch_202610”

## 30-day experiment (no invented forecasts)
- Week 1: verify production /pro and registration, validate source/UTM counts, fix broken links.
- Week 2: publish 3 useful buyer-intent comparisons, each backed by dated evidence, with a relevant /pro CTA.
- Week 3: use a small owner-reviewed Pinterest/YouTube/TikTok/X pilot linking to the corresponding BlindBoxAI research page; no affiliate-link dump.
- Week 4: compare verified opt-ins to attributable qualified visits; interview **voluntary** participants through permitted opt-in communications only, testing which decision hurts most. No claims about signups/revenue without data.

## Quality gate
A winning alpha experience demonstrably helps users avoid one bad purchase or compute a margin correctly. Track activation and intent. Do not build login or recurring billing on the assumption that a waitlist means validated demand. Test willingness to pay, churn, and usage before charging.
