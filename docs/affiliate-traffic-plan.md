# Affiliate Traffic Execution Plan

## Goal
Turn manually generated NotebookLM videos into measurable BlindBoxAI traffic and eBay Partner Network clicks with minimal manual handling.

## Funnel
NotebookLM video -> social post -> BlindBoxAI verified series page -> direct EPN affiliate link -> eBay.

## Required attribution
- Use BlindBoxAI landing URLs in captions/CTAs.
- Keep EPN campaign ID in environment configuration, never in source.
- Use eBay Custom IDs on outbound links for product/placement attribution.
- Preserve clear affiliate disclosure in captions and any visual/audible disclosure required for video by current EPN/platform rules.

## Metrics to monitor
1. Social post views/reach.
2. BlindBoxAI page visits.
3. BlindBoxAI outbound affiliate clicks.
4. EPN clicks and transactions by campaign/custom ID.
5. Earnings per click and conversion rate once volume exists.

## Operating cadence
- Generate/download one useful collector video.
- Ingest and host from Termux.
- Review once.
- Publish through Buffer.
- Review click/commission analytics regularly and double down on topics producing outbound clicks rather than raw views alone.
