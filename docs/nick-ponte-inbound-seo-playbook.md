Source note: added from the owner's Nick Ponte research queue on 2026-08-22. Treat this as an inspiration source for traffic, buyer intent, and conversion structure. Do not copy creator content, promises, scripts, thumbnails, or unverified claims.

Use this playbook to turn verified blind-box demand into pages, scripts, and tracking paths that bring collectors and buyers to BlindBoxAI.com.

This asset will convert verified collector demand into searchable, trackable BlindBoxAI pages so that traffic, affiliate clicks, sponsor interest, and buyer confidence can grow without unsafe claims or manual chasing.

## Approved tactic filter

Use only tactics that pass all filters:

- Buyer intent: the topic matches a real collector question, search phrase, or decision point.
- Monetization path: the page can route to BlindBoxAI.com, EPN-compliant affiliate clicks, sponsor interest, or email capture.
- Evidence standard: facts are backed by official brand pages, marketplace policy-safe transaction evidence, or reviewed BlindBoxAI data.
- Compliance: no guarantee of profit, authenticity, resale value, rarity, or zero risk.
- Ownership: create original BlindBoxAI framing; do not reuse another creator's private material.
- Safety: no outreach, purchase, bid, payment, account action, or publishing without owner approval.

Reject tactics when they depend on hype-only claims, unverifiable revenue numbers, fake scarcity, manipulative urgency, scraping private content, or raw affiliate links outside the approved redirect path.

## Content structure for each page

Use this order for programmatic or manually staged pages:

1. Plain-English answer to the collector's question.
2. Evidence box with source type, timestamp, and what was verified.
3. Price or demand range only when the data supports it.
4. Authenticity and risk notes before any affiliate CTA.
5. BlindBoxAI CTA, routed through approved tracking such as `/api/out/ebay` where applicable.
6. Short FAQ based on Mr. Know It All demand themes.
7. Update date and evidence limitations.

## Video script extraction

For every approved page, create one short video angle:

- Hook: one collector decision question, not a profit promise.
- Proof: one verified fact or safe price context.
- Warning: one authenticity, condition, or evidence limitation.
- CTA: "Check the guide at BlindBoxAI.com" or another approved BlindBoxAI-owned destination.
- Disclosure: include required affiliate or sponsor disclosure when applicable.

Avoid claims like "guaranteed flip," "easy money," "you must buy now," or "this will rise in value."

## Private owner report additions

When a demand candidate is reviewed, include these fields in the private owner report:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `search_intent` | The collector question or buyer-intent phrase the asset answers |
| `monetization_path` | Affiliate click, sponsor page, email capture, or buyer-confidence page |
| `asset_type` | Series page, comparison page, authenticity guide, video script, or FAQ |
| `evidence_gap` | Missing proof that blocks publishing |
| `approved_cta` | The exact BlindBoxAI-owned CTA destination |
| `tracking_plan` | Custom ID or analytics event needed to measure performance |

## Weekly execution loop

1. Pull Mr. Know It All demand themes.
2. Select up to five high-intent page angles.
3. Verify evidence and reject weak opportunities.
4. Stage only the strongest pages or scripts for owner review.
5. Publish approved assets.
6. Review affiliate custom ID rollups and page analytics.
7. Double down only on pages with real traffic, clicks, sponsor interest, or repeated collector demand.

## Success metrics

Track these without storing personal visitor data:

- organic visits by page type;
- CTA clicks by custom ID;
- video-to-page click lift;
- sponsor/contact form starts;
- repeated Mr. Know It All question themes;
- pages that earn clicks but need better evidence or clearer CTAs.
