# BlindBoxAI Affiliate Money Magnet

## Objective
Turn existing collectible research and buyer questions into inbound commercial-intent traffic, while preserving BlindBoxAI's verified-data, EPN disclosure, no-purchasing, and owner-control requirements.

## Revenue loop
Research signals -> opportunity score -> buyer-intent asset -> BlindBoxAI.com -> attributable outbound EPN click -> provider-confirmed conversion/revenue -> winner replication.

Clicks are never treated as sales.

## Priority opportunity score
Rank candidate collectibles/content using only available, attributable evidence:
- commercial/buyer intent
- verified demand/interest signals
- content gap
- existing BlindBoxAI engagement
- attributable outbound CTR
- provider-confirmed conversion/revenue when available
- freshness/availability of evidence

Do not invent search volume, prices, scarcity, conversion rates, or revenue.

## Buyer-intent asset families
1. Current value / price research
2. Character or series comparisons
3. Authenticity / identification
4. Rarity and collector guides
5. Where-to-find / active opportunity pages
6. Is-it-worth-it research

Every affiliate-bearing page must provide useful pre-sell information before an outbound link and retain required affiliate disclosures.

## Winner replication
When a topic demonstrates attributable commercial behavior, prioritize adjacent assets rather than blindly increasing content volume. Revenue and provider-confirmed conversions outrank CTR; CTR outranks raw views.

## Pinterest pilot
Pinterest is an inbound visual-search distribution channel, not a raw affiliate-link dump.

For each approved high-opportunity collectible, generate Pinterest-ready concepts such as:
- visual value guide
- character identification card
- series comparison
- rarity guide
- collector checklist
- price/research graphic

Destination: a relevant BlindBoxAI.com page. Never place raw eBay/EPN listing URLs in Pinterest creative. Use only images/assets whose use is authorized and keep claims evidence-based.

Initial pilot should be small and measurable. Track source/campaign/content identifiers at the BlindBoxAI landing page and compare qualified visits and attributable outbound behavior before scaling.

## Owned-audience layer
Offer an optional BlindBox Watchlist for users who want updates on selected series/collectibles. Collect the minimum necessary information, make consent explicit, and do not promise unavailable real-time data.

## Mr. Know It All feedback loop
Aggregate privacy-safe question intent into content candidates. Prioritize repeated questions with commercial intent. Do not expose private owner analytics or grant the public agent spending/purchasing authority.

## Sponsor readiness
Measure sponsor-safe aggregate evidence: qualified traffic, top content/series, repeat engagement, video performance, and voluntary subscriber counts. Sponsorship claims must be based on measured data and disclosed when published content is sponsored.

## Automation boundary
Safe to automate: scoring from existing verified signals, content candidate queues, attribution metadata, refresh reminders, Pinterest creative briefs, and owner dashboard summaries.

Keep gated/manual where required: unverified factual claims, new external publishing credentials/channels, sponsored claims, policy-sensitive affiliate changes, and any action that could create financial liability.

## Tonight launch gate
1. Existing production remains stable.
2. Build/test/security gates pass.
3. Affiliate disclosures and attribution remain intact.
4. No raw eBay links are introduced into social/video distribution.
5. Pinterest pilot destinations resolve to BlindBoxAI.com.
6. Owner dashboard can distinguish visits/clicks from provider-confirmed revenue.
7. Deploy only after the branch passes the repository's existing checks.

## Tooling preference
Prefer existing production services and first-party platform capabilities over adding paid dependencies. GitHub remains the source/change-control plane and Vercel the production deployment plane. Add a new service only when it removes material manual work or produces measurable incremental revenue without weakening security/compliance.
