BLINDBOXAI EVIDENCE INTAKE

CONTRIBUTOR ROUTE

/contribute

The contributor route is marked noindex and is protected by
a private access code.

ACCEPTED RIGHTS BASES

A contributor must confirm one of the following:

- They took the photographs.
- They own the photograph copyright.
- They have written permission from the copyright owner.
- The photographs carry a verified reuse license.

Affiliate participation does not automatically grant the
right to copy marketplace listing photographs.

PRIVATE STORAGE

Uploaded photographs are stored under:

evidence/uploads/SUBMISSION-ID/

Pending submission records are stored under:

evidence/manifests/pending/SUBMISSION-ID.json

All new evidence remains private until human review.

ADMINISTRATION COMMANDS

blindboxai-evidence doctor

blindboxai-evidence list

blindboxai-evidence fetch SUBMISSION_ID

blindboxai-evidence show-code

PUBLICATION RULE

No submitted photograph is automatically published.

Before publication, a human must review:

1. Copyright, permission, or license basis.
2. Required photographer attribution.
3. Privacy and verification-code redaction.
4. Authenticity or provenance evidence.
5. Series and figure identification.
6. Caption accuracy.
7. Preview rendering.

Visual differences must be described as warning signs,
not automatic proof that an item is counterfeit.
