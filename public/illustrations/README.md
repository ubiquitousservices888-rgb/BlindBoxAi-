# Site illustrations

Drop original illustrations here (webp or png, about 1200x900 for cards,
1600x1200 for the hero), then point the matching slot in
`lib/site-images.mjs` at the file. Until a slot has a file, the site shows
its own drawn placeholder, so nothing ever renders as a broken image.

Slot names: hero, category-blind-boxes, category-cards, category-coins,
guide-fake-check, guide-mew-ex, tool-buy-or-pass, tool-resale-margin,
accessory/<offer id>, series/<series slug>.

Rules: original art or the owner's own photos only. No brand logos, no
marketplace listing photos, no copyrighted product shots.
