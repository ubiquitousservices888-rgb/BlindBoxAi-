## Context from independent sources

1. The Bureau of Engraving and Printing confirms the Series 1976 $2 Federal Reserve note was introduced for the Bicentennial, with Jefferson on front and the Declaration of Independence scene on reverse: https://www.bep.gov/currency/circulating-currency/2-note
2. BEP history says the $2 note was reintroduced in 1976 on Jefferson's birthday (April 13): https://www.bep.gov/currency/history
3. The American Numismatic Association documents post offices canceling 13-cent stamps on notes on April 13, 1976; such souvenirs were produced in large numbers. **An April 13 cancel does not by itself prove rarity or exceptional value.** https://www.money.org/video/1976-2-dollar-bill/
4. The US Currency Education Program documents features of $2 notes for visual checks. Photos cannot substitute for physical authentication: https://www.uscurrency.gov/denominations/2
