Five checks before selling

  1. Photograph both sides in even light, with the full edge visible.
  2. Read the date and mint mark. Record “not visible” rather than guessing.
  3. Weigh the coin on a precise scale. A Morgan silver dollar originally weighed 26.73 grams.
  4. Inspect wear, cleaning, scratches, corrosion, and mounts. Never clean a collectible coin.
  5. Compare recent completed sales for the same date, mint, condition, and problems—not asking prices.
Why the mint mark matters: A 1899 Philadelphia Morgan can be much more collectible than an 1899-O. Misreading a tiny mark can create a misleading price claim.
