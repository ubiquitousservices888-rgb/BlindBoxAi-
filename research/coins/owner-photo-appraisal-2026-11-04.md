## Appraisal safeguards
1. Require paired obverse and reverse photos, date, and mint mark evidence. Record **not visible** rather than guessing.
2. Never infer a mint mark from the wreath or from image enhancement alone. The 1879 Morgan was incorrectly called 1879-O and then corrected to likely Philadelphia.
3. Do not represent broad conversational estimates as verified sales comparables. Refresh silver spot prices and collect actual dated sold transactions before publishing a price range.
4. Flag mounting, scratches, possible cleaning, corrosion, damage, and worn dates; no automatic claims of mint errors or rare varieties.
5. For Morgan dollars, standard specification is 26.73 g and 90% silver; weight alone cannot authenticate. 1964 and 1961 half dollars are 90% silver; 1966 Kennedy half dollar is 40% silver.
6. No cleaning or acid-based date restoration recommendations.
7. Human approval required before publishing to YouTube, TikTok, website, or affiliate pages. Preserve Yellow review / Blue owner approval gates.
8. No affiliate links or transaction claims until attribution, disclosure, and destination URLs are independently checked.
